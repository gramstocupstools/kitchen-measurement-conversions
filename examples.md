
# Practical Kitchen Measurement Examples

## Example 1: Convert Flour Grams to Cups

A recipe requires 250 grams of all-purpose flour.

Reference weight: 120 grams per US cup.

Calculation:

250 / 120 = 2.0833 cups

Result: Approximately 2.08 US cups.

## Example 2: Convert Sugar Cups to Grams

A recipe requires 1.5 cups of granulated sugar.

Reference weight: 198 grams per US cup.

Calculation:

1.5 * 198 = 297 grams

Result: Approximately 297 grams.

## Example 3: Convert Butter Cups to Grams

A recipe requires half a cup of butter.

Reference weight: 226 grams per US cup.

Calculation:

0.5 * 226 = 113 grams

Result: Approximately 113 grams.

## Example 4: Convert Honey Grams to Cups

A recipe requires 100 grams of honey.

Reference weight: 336 grams per US cup.

Calculation:

100 / 336 = 0.2976 cups

Result: Approximately 0.30 US cups.

## Example 5: Convert Dry Basmati Rice Grams to Cups

A recipe requires 250 grams of dry white basmati rice.

Working reference: 185 grams per US cup.

Calculation:

250 / 185 = 1.3514 cups

Result: Approximately 1.35 US cups.

This rice conversion uses a provisional reference.
Measure your particular product for greater accuracy.

## Additional Considerations

- Use a digital kitchen scale for greater accuracy.
- Always select the correct ingredient.
- Check whether rice is dry or cooked.
- Avoid compressing ingredients unless specified.
- Use the measuring cup standard required by the recipe.
- Round only the final calculated result.

All values are approximate kitchen reference measurements.
